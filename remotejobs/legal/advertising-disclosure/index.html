RemoteJobs Advertising Disclosure

Applies to the RemoteJobs app · Last updated 8 September 2026

Contents

  1. Advertising in this app
  2. No advertising identifiers and no ad profiling
  3. Rewarded video
  4. Paid placements are a separate matter

1. Advertising in this app

RemoteJobs does not show third-party advertising. No advertising network is integrated into the application, and no advertising SDK is included in it.

2. No advertising identifiers and no ad profiling

Because no ad network is present, we do not collect an advertising identifier, we do not build an advertising profile about you, and we do not share your data with advertising or ad-measurement providers.

3. Rewarded video

The app contains a rewarded-video surface used during development to simulate unlocking temporary access. It is a simulation with no advertisement behind it, and it is not available in the released application. If real rewarded advertising is introduced later, this document will be updated before that version is released, and what it unlocks and what data is used will be described here.

4. Paid placements are a separate matter

Sponsored placements and affiliate links, where they exist, are not advertising networks and are covered by the Sponsored Listing Disclosure and the Affiliate Disclosure.

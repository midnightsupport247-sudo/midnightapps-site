1. What this discloses

The Remote Work Shop area of RemoteJobs can contain links to third-party products and services. Where we have an affiliate relationship with a provider, we may earn a commission if you buy through that link, at no additional cost to you. Where such a relationship exists it is disclosed in the app at the point you see the link, in addition to this document. Affiliate arrangements never affect the job listings you are shown or the order in which they appear.

2. What we do not do

We do not accept payment to place a job listing higher in search results, and we do not treat an affiliate relationship as an endorsement. We do not own, operate, test or inspect the third-party products, services or providers linked from the Shop, and we make no promise about their quality, price, availability or results.

3. Buying from a third party

Any purchase you make through such a link is a contract between you and that third party, on their site and under their terms — including their pricing, delivery, cancellation and refund terms. Prices and availability can change without notice. Questions about an order go to that provider, not to us.

4. Sponsored content

Anything we are paid to promote is labelled as sponsored where it appears, as well as being covered by this disclosure. See the Sponsored Listing Disclosure.

5. Contact

Questions about this disclosure: legal@midnightapps.dev.

RemoteJobs Free-Trial Terms

1. When these terms apply

These terms apply only if a free trial is offered to you for a RemoteJobs subscription. Whether a trial is available depends on your plan, your country and your store account, and the App Store or Google Play decides your eligibility — usually allowing one trial per store account per product. If no trial is shown on the purchase screen, none is being offered to you.

2. What the trial includes

A trial gives you the same subscriber access as the paid plan it belongs to, for the trial length shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. That screen, and not any figure elsewhere in the app or in this document, states the length and the price that follows.

3. What happens when it ends

Unless you cancel before the trial ends, it converts automatically into a paid subscription and the store charges you the price shown at signup. The subscription then renews automatically like any other. You are not charged during the trial itself.

4. Cancelling before you are charged

Cancel through your store account at least 24 hours before the trial ends: on iOS, in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions; on Android, in the Google Play app, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. The subscription screen in the app links you there. You keep trial access until the trial period ends.

5. If you are charged unexpectedly

Because the store processes the payment, a refund request for a charge you did not expect goes to the store. Contact midnightsupport247@gmail.com as well and we will help you raise it and check your entitlement record.

1. What a subscription gives you

RemoteJobs offers a paid subscription that unlocks subscriber-only job data, including full listing details and the employer's application link. A free account can browse the catalogue with limited access. Your entitlement is verified on our servers against what the store reports, so access reflects a real purchase rather than a setting on your device.

2. Plans and price

Subscriptions are offered on a monthly and a yearly plan. The price, currency and billing period that apply to you are the ones shown on the purchase screen and confirmed by the App Store or Google Play at the moment you subscribe. Prices vary by country and may be adjusted by the store; the store's confirmation, not any figure displayed elsewhere, is what you are charged.

3. Who bills you

All payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on where you installed the app. We never receive or store your payment card details. Your purchase is also subject to that store's own terms and conditions.

4. Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, the store will charge you for the next period at the then-current price and your subscription will continue. Renewal is managed entirely by the store; we cannot charge you ourselves, and we cannot renew a subscription the store has ended.

5. Cancelling

You cancel through your store account: on iOS, in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions; on Android, in the Google Play app, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. The app links you to that screen from its subscription settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Deleting your RemoteJobs account does not cancel billing with the store, so cancel first if you no longer want to be charged.

6. Free trials and introductory offers

Where a free trial or introductory offer is available for your plan and country, the store shows its exact length and the price that follows before you confirm, and those are the terms that apply. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, it converts automatically into a paid subscription at the price shown at signup. Eligibility is determined by the store, typically once per store account. See the Free Trial Terms.

7. Price changes

If we change a subscription price, the store will notify you and, where the store requires it, ask for your consent before the new price takes effect. You can cancel before the change applies if you do not wish to continue.

8. Taxes

Prices shown by the store include or exclude tax according to that store's rules for your country. The store calculates and remits any applicable sales tax or VAT.

9. If your entitlement does not arrive

If you have been charged but the app does not show your subscription, use Restore purchases on the subscription screen. If that does not resolve it, contact support@midnightapps.dev with the store receipt and we will investigate. We do not grant paid access on the basis of an unverified claim, because entitlement is checked against the store's record.

10. Changes to the paid feature set

We may add to, or change, what a subscription includes. We will not materially reduce what you have already paid for during a period you have paid for. The continued availability of any particular job listing is not part of what the subscription guarantees — listings come from third parties and close without notice.

11. Contact

Billing questions: support@midnightapps.dev.

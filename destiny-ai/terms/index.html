2. What Destiny AI is

Destiny AI turns the birth details you enter into readings: a natal chart and astrology reports, numerology profiles, Chinese astrology readings, astrocartography maps of places associated with different themes, and daily, weekly, monthly and yearly outlooks. Readings are generated by the app's software and written content from traditional astrological and numerological systems. The same birth details always produce the same readings; they are not written for you by a person or by an AI service.

3. Entertainment only — not professional advice

Destiny AI is intended for entertainment and self-reflection. Astrology, numerology, Chinese astrology and astrocartography are not scientifically validated, and nothing in the app guarantees or predicts any outcome.

Nothing in Destiny AI is medical, mental-health, legal, financial, investment, tax, relationship or other professional advice. Do not use a reading to make decisions about your health, safety, money, relationships, career, or where to live or travel. For those decisions, speak to a qualified professional. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency services.

6. Free features and Destiny AI Premium

Many features are free, including your daily reading, your natal chart, viewing and searching the astrocartography map, the transits report, the yearly forecast, Chinese compatibility, your daily history, the Learn library, and saving up to five locations and five readings.

Destiny AI Premium unlocks the full natal, numerology, astrocartography and Chinese astrology reports, weekly and monthly outlooks, detailed city insights, location comparison and travel tools on the map, voice playback, PDF export of reports, and unlimited saved locations and readings. The features included in Premium may change as the app develops; the subscription screen shows what is included.

7. Subscriptions and billing

  • Plans. Destiny AI Premium is an auto-renewing subscription with a Monthly plan, which renews every month, and a Yearly plan, which renews every year. In the United States the prices are $5.99 per month and $49.99 per year. Prices in other countries are set in local currency and may differ; the price for your App Store country is shown in the app before you confirm.
  • Billing by Apple. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store and are subject to Apple's terms. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase, or when your free trial ends if you started with one.
  • Automatic renewal. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple ID account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Cancelling. Manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad). Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Premium until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID account settings to stop future charges.
  • Restore Purchases. If you reinstall the app or move to a new device signed in to the same Apple ID, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen to restore an active subscription.
  • Price changes. Subscription prices may change. Price changes follow Apple's App Store rules, under which Apple notifies affected subscribers before a price increase applies to them.

8. Free trial

  • Eligible new subscribers may be offered a 7-day free trial of the Monthly or Yearly plan. The app shows a free trial only when the App Store reports that one is available to you.
  • Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it converts automatically to a paid subscription and your Apple ID account is charged the price of the plan you chose.
  • Free-trial eligibility is determined by Apple. Apple allows one introductory offer, including a free trial, per subscription group, so if you have already used one for Destiny AI Premium you may not be offered another.
  • If you buy a subscription while a free trial is active, any unused portion of the trial is forfeited when the paid subscription begins.

About the studio

A small studio with a narrow focus

Midnight Apps exists to do one thing well: ship consumer mobile applications that people actually keep on their phones. We work on a deliberately short list of products at a time, which means design, engineering, and support all stay in the same hands rather than being handed down a chain.

The studio takes its name from the hours when most of this work happens — quiet, focused, and unhurried. That preference shows up in the products: calm interfaces, no dark patterns, no attention-farming, and no data collection we can't justify to the person it belongs to.

Product & design

Interface design, interaction detail, accessibility, and copy are treated as part of the product rather than a coat of paint applied at the end.

Mobile engineering

Native-feeling applications built for iOS and Android, engineered for the constraints that matter on a phone: battery, offline use, small screens, and slow networks.

Privacy by default

We collect the minimum a feature needs to function, keep data on the device wherever that is workable, and publish a plain-language privacy policy for every product we release.

What we work on

Mobile application development

Everything the studio does points at one platform family: applications that run in someone's pocket. That focus decides the tools, the review standards, and the kinds of problems we take on.

iOS and Android

Applications designed for release through the Apple App Store and Google Play, built to each platform's human interface conventions rather than one layout stretched across both.

Health, wellness, and daily-use tools

Practical companions for routines people already follow — tracking, scheduling, reminders, and reference material — where accuracy and clarity matter more than novelty.

Offline-first data handling

Apps that keep working on a plane, in a basement, or on a bad connection, and that treat syncing as an enhancement rather than a requirement.

Long-term maintenance

Annual OS releases, deprecations, store policy changes, and bug reports do not stop after launch. Ongoing upkeep is planned into every product from the start.

In development — not yet released

MyPeptide — The GLP-1 & Peptide Companion

A companion app for people following a GLP-1 or peptide protocol prescribed by their clinician. MyPeptide is being built to organize medication schedules, injection history and site rotation, supply and refill tracking, progress notes, and educational material in one calm, private place.

MyPeptide is an organizational and educational tool. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medication, or decide whether a treatment is right for anyone — those conversations belong with a qualified healthcare professional.
